? "I'm healthy. I don't need this yet."

? "I already have company HMO."

That's ₱88/day, less than most Grab rides. And unlike any expense, at Year 7 you get ~85% of it back. It's not spending. It's a savings plan that protects you while you save.

Kaiser International Healthgroup, Inc. is an actuarially-sound, licensed HMO in the Philippines with 500+ accredited hospitals and a 15+ year track record. Insurance underwriting is done by a duly authorized insurance company.
FAQ

Questions you should be asking.

Straight answers to the big life questions this plan is designed to solve.

1
What if I die too soon? Who will take care of my family?
The solution is Life Insurance — instant money. Life insurance protection can help you replace your income, finance your children's education, pay estate tax, settle debts, and ensure your family is taken care of even without you.
2
What if I get sick? Who will take care of me?
The solution is Healthcare. When you retire or stop working, you need someone to take care of you. How comfortable your healthcare situation is after the age of 60 depends entirely on the decision you make today. The Kaiser K-45 plan guarantees lifetime healthcare so you are never left without coverage.
3
What if I live too long? Who will take care of me?
The solution is Investment — letting money work for you. The Kaiser plan builds a Health Savings Account that generates continuous income for your retirement. With projected cash values reaching ₱2,917,710 at age 60 and ₱4,699,001 at age 65, your money keeps growing long after you stop paying.
4
Is it transferrable or can it be assigned?
The Kaiser Ultimate Health Builder Plan cannot be sold, assigned, or transferred to any third party, nor can payment of the membership fee be assumed by a third party.
5
Is my Kaiser Health Plan transferable after my passing?
Yes. Your Kaiser Health Plan is transferable once the policy holder is deceased. If the proposed member is underage and has already reached 18 years old, the proposed member and payor must submit their duly accomplished deed of assignment and membership application form with valid ID to transfer the policy.
